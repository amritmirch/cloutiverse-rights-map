Names and roles are the real credits. The percentages are examples, because this song's actual split isn't public. Drag them to try other splits.

Every contributor signs the same split sheet. The fingerprint below is a real SHA-256 of that sheet, so any change produces a new one. In this demo nothing is sent to a blockchain.

Total paid out

YouTube views are the real count on Sirasa TV's official video as of 1 Oct 2026. Streams and the TV fee are examples, since those numbers aren't public. Assumes about US$0.003 per stream and US$0.0008 per view at LKR 300 per dollar. Streaming and YouTube income goes 80% to the master and 20% to the composition. TV fees and licensed-work royalties split 50/50.

Grant a license for a new work built on this track. The new work joins the map and pays a royalty back to every rights holder on the original, automatically.
